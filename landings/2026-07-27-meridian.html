Where precision meets poetry

Meridian is an independent watchmaking atelier in the Swiss Jura. Every timepiece is conceived, machined, and assembled by hand — a marriage of centuries-old tradition and modern engineering.

Discover collections Our approach

Time made tangible

In an age of disposable goods, we believe in permanence. Every Meridian watch carries the touch of the hand that assembled it — from the first gear to the final polish. Our movements are Swiss-made, our cases are machined from solid brass and stainless steel, and every dial is finished by hand.

We do not chase trends. We refine the essential: a mechanical movement that is accurate, robust, and beautiful; a case that sits naturally on the wrist; a design that speaks in whispers, not shouts. Each piece takes twelve to eighteen months from conception to delivery.

— Founded by Henri Vallier, master watchmaker

Our collections

The art of making

Every Meridian watch passes through four stages of creation, each one guided by a master artisan. No piece is assembled twice in the same way.

⚙

Design & engineering

Each calibre is designed in-house using parametric modelling. Gears, bridges, and mainplates are stress-tested before a single chip is cut.

⛽

Machining & finishing

Cases are turned from solid 316L stainless steel. Bridges are bevelled and perlage-finished by hand under a microscope. Every surface receives attention.

⚘

Assembly & regulation

A single watchmaker builds each movement from blank mainplate to ticking assembly — an intensive process that spans two to three weeks.

☆

Testing & certification

Every watch is regulated in five positions over 48 hours, pressure-tested, and worn for seven days before it leaves the atelier.

From nitrate to 4K

Restoration is a form of respect.

Every film in our catalogue passes through the same four rooms of our lab. The work is manual, slow, and deliberately invisible.

  1. Scan

    Negatives and fine-grain masters are scanned at 4K and 8K, frame by frame, on wet-gate scanners that read through surface damage.

  2. Restore

    Tears, dirt and flicker are repaired by hand. Where material is lost, the program note says so. No invented detail. No AI.

  3. Grade

    Colour is graded against period references — Technicolor charts, release prints, the chemists’ own notes. Never a modern look.

  4. Project

    We present the theatrical cut with the original score and silence intact. The film you watch is the film that premiered.

Questions, answered

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. Your phone alone works for plans and coaching. A heart-rate strap or watch unlocks zone training and recovery scoring.

How does the plan adapt?

After every session, Ritmo reads how your body actually responded — zones, pace, feel — and reshapes the coming week. Miss a run? The plan rebalances instead of punishing you.

Can I import my training history?

Yes. One-click import from the major platforms, or a quick manual baseline if you're starting fresh.

Is there a free tier?

Starter is free forever — one adaptive plan, zone coaching and three workouts a week. Pro adds the race planner, recovery intelligence and cadence lab.

Does Ritmo work for cycling too?

Absolutely. Runners, riders and duathletes all use Ritmo. Plans adapt to your sport — and to mixing both across a season.

What happens after the trial?

You keep your Starter plan for free. Upgrade to Pro any time — no lock-in, cancel in two taps.

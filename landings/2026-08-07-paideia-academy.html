Our conviction
"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire." — W. B. Yeats

The humanities are not a luxury. They are the practice of paying attention — to language, to history, to each other. In an age of endless distraction, we believe the most radical act is to sit with a difficult text and think together.

Our seminars are small, our questions are open, and our standards are high. Every course is led by a scholar who has devoted their life to the subject. No prerecorded lectures, no multiple-choice quizzes — just conversation, close reading, and the slow work of understanding.

How it works

Three movements, one journey.

Every course follows the same rhythm. You move through each week at your own pace, guided by the text and your seminar leader.

1

Read deeply.

One primary text per week. No abridgments, no summaries — the real thing. You receive a reading guide with context, questions, and related passages. Read at your pace, in your space.

2

Write briefly.

A short weekly response — not a paper, but a genuine attempt to think on the page. Your seminar leader reads every one and weaves your ideas into the discussion.

3

Discuss together.

A live 90-minute seminar each week with no more than twelve readers. Not a lecture — a conversation. You leave with more questions than you arrived with, and that is the point.

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first board.

Do I need design experience?

None at all. MORADA works like a pinboard — drag things in, move them around, see what feels right. The palette tool does the color math for you.

Can I share a board with someone who doesn't have an account?

Yes. Every board gets a private link — guests can view and comment without creating an account. Only the board owner decides who can edit.

Where do the furniture prices come from?

Studio and Team plans include a live price book sourced from major retailers. Prices update weekly, so your plan budget stays realistic.

Can my architect see my boards?

If you share the link, yes — and most architects love it. A board full of your pinned taste is worth more than a thousand words in a briefing call.

Is my data private?

Boards are private by default and never appear in search. We don't sell data, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in two clicks. Your boards stay readable on the Free plan — you only lose the extra collaborators and tools.

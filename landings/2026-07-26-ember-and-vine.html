Our Ethos

Rooted in the Seasons

Every dish at Ember & Vine begins with the land. We work directly with small farms, foragers, and fisheries who share our reverence for ingredient integrity. Our menu changes with the rhythm of the seasons — not out of trend, but out of respect.

The hearth is our heart. Wood-fire cooking imparts a depth that gas cannot replicate — a primal connection to the element that has gathered humans around meals for millennia. Each ember, each vine, carries the memory of the sun that grew it.

The Visionary

Chef Elena Voss

Elena Voss learned the language of ingredients on her grandmother's farm in Tuscany and refined her craft in Michelin-starred kitchens across Copenhagen, San Sebastian, and Melbourne. At Ember & Vine, she brings that global perspective to a single, essential question: what does this season taste like?

Her cooking is intuitive yet precise — each plate a quiet composition of texture, temperature, and memory. She believes the best meals are the ones that make you pause, breathe, and notice the world around you.

— Elena Voss

Craft & Heritage

Made by hand,
built to last.

Every AURA piece begins in our atelier in the hills of Tuscany, where master pattern cutters and sewists work with fabrics that have been selected over months, not weeks. We use traditional tailoring techniques — hand-finished hems, reinforced seams, and meticulous quality checks at every stage — because a garment that endures is the most sustainable choice of all.

Principles

A better way
to dress.

Luxury and responsibility are not mutually exclusive. We prove it through three commitments that shape every decision we make.

Responsible Materials

We source only organic cotton, mulesing-free wool, certified linen, and traceable cashmere. Every fiber is chosen for its environmental integrity as much as its hand feel.

Timeless Design

We do not follow trends. Our silhouettes are refined over years, not seasons — grounded in proportion, utility, and quiet beauty. A garment you will reach for again and again.

Radical Transparency

Every garment comes with a digital passport tracing its journey from field to finished piece. We publish our supplier list. We show you the true cost of making things well.

Sustainability

The full cost
of making things well.

We believe in radical transparency. Every AURA garment carries a digital passport that traces its full journey — from farm to spinning mill to dye house to our atelier to your wardrobe. We publish our supply chain, our carbon footprint per piece, and the true cost breakdown. Not because we are perfect, but because honesty is the only foundation for change.

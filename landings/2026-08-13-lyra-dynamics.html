The Aurora constellation

Twelve satellites in a dawn-synchronous orbit, imaging the entire planet every 45 minutes. No clouds, no borders, no blind spots.

Built to scan, not to drift

Aurora is a working network, not a demonstration. Each satellite carries a multispectral imager with a 0.3 m ground sample distance, cross-linked to its neighbors so a task anywhere becomes an image anywhere.

  • ✓0.3 m ground resolution — vehicles, vessels, and infrastructure readable from orbit
  • ✓45-minute global revisit via dawn-synchronous phasing
  • ✓98.6° inclination — pole-to-pole coverage, no exclusion zones

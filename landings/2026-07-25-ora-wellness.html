Support

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about Ora. If you don't see your question, reach out to our support team.

After you complete a brief assessment covering your needs, preferences, and goals, our matching algorithm pairs you with a licensed therapist who specialises in your areas of concern. You can review their profile before booking your first session, and switching is always free if it doesn't feel like the right fit.
Absolutely. Ora is HIPAA-compliant and uses end-to-end encryption for all communications, including video sessions, messaging, and stored data. Your information is never shared with third parties. We believe privacy is essential to effective care, and we've built our platform around that principle from day one.
Yes, and it's completely free. We understand that the therapeutic relationship is deeply personal. You can request a new therapist at any time through your dashboard. Your previous sessions remain private, and you can start fresh with a new provider who better matches your needs.
We accept a growing number of insurance plans across the United States. During sign-up, you can enter your insurance details to check coverage in real time. We also offer competitive out-of-pocket rates and a financial assistance program for qualifying individuals. No one is turned away due to financial circumstances.
Ora is not a crisis service. If you're experiencing a mental health emergency, please call your local emergency number (988 in the US) or go to your nearest emergency room. Our Premium plan includes a crisis response line for urgent situations, but for immediate danger, always contact emergency services first.

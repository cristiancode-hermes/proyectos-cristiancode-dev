How a tanda works

The same rhythm your family has used for generations — with a bank-grade ledger underneath.

01

Form your circle

Invite 5–25 people you trust. Agree on the monthly contribution and how the pot is ordered — rotation, lottery, or auction.

02

Contribute monthly

Tanda collects from everyone automatically and holds each pot in a regulated escrow account. Reminders handle the rest.

03

Receive your pot

On your month, the full pot lands in your account — no delays, no awkward conversations.

The idea

A festival built around one long night of light.

Solstice isn't a lineup stapled to a field. It's a single, continuous piece of sound — programmed like a mixtape that peaks with the sun. From the first chord at golden hour to the ambient closer as the sky turns violet, every act is placed for a reason, and the crowd moves as one body through the night.

Questions

Before you pack the tent.

Everything people usually ask. Something else on your mind?

Still curious?

hello@solstice.fest — we reply within 24h.

Yes — bring your own food, and water in reusable bottles. Glass containers and alcohol are not allowed inside the arena, but the campsite has a full market with local vendors and a 24h kitchen.

Shuttle buses run from Sierra Norte station every 20 minutes throughout the weekend, included in your ticket. If you drive, buy a parking pass in advance — spaces sell out weeks ahead.

Hot days (28–32°C) and cool nights (12–15°C). Bring a warm layer for the afterhours stage and sunscreen for the solar stage. There are shade zones and misting stations across the site.

Half our crowd comes solo. There's a dedicated solo-camper zone, a meet-up on Friday afternoon, and volunteers patrolling 24/7. The valley is a safe, friendly place to meet people.

Full refunds until May 1. After that, tickets are transferable — you can change the name on a ticket for free up to 48h before the gates open.

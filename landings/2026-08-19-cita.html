How it works

Scheduling that runs itself.

Set rules once; CITA handles the rest.

01

Set your availability

Tell CITA your working hours once. Recurring rules, buffer time and timezones just work.

02

Share one link

Drop your CITA link anywhere — email sig, Slack, your site. No apps for the person booking.

03

Get booked, automatically

Invitees pick a slot that respects your rules. Double-bookings are impossible, reminders are sent for you.

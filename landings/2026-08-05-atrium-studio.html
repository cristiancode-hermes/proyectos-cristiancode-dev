Residential · 2025

Casa Serra — Hillside Retreat

A concrete-and-glass residence carved into the Catalan hillside. The brief called for a home that disappears into its landscape — every room opens onto a private courtyard or the valley beyond. Local stone, exposed board-formed concrete, and Mediterranean oak define the material palette.

Commercial · 2024

Scribe — Coworking & Library

A workspace that reads like a private library. Volume, silence, and deep oak.

Interior · 2025

Plaça Major Apartment

A 120m² apartment in an 18th-century Eixample building. Original ceiling mouldings meet custom joinery in warm grey and sage.

Cultural · 2024

Can Framis Pavilion

A 400m² exhibition pavilion within the Can Framis museum grounds. The structure is a single folded concrete plane that lifts at one corner — creating a shaded entrance and a sculptural silhouette.

How we work

From conversation to cornerstone

We keep the process human — no templates, no delivery-room formalism. Three movements, from the first conversation to the finished space.

Listen and Observe

We spend time on site — watching the light, feeling the wind, understanding how you actually live or work. This phase lasts as long as it needs to.

Draw and Refine

Physical models, hand sketches, and full-scale mock-ups. We test proportions with our hands before committing to construction drawings.

Build and Inhabit

We stay on site through construction — working alongside artisans, adjusting details, and ensuring the finished space holds the quiet conviction of the original idea.

Our studio
"Architecture is not about form. It is about the quality of the space between walls — the light, the sound, the feeling of arrival."

Founded in 2012, Atrium is a Barcelona-based studio of twelve architects, designers, and makers. We work across scales — from a single piece of furniture to an entire neighborhood — but our concern is always the same: how a space shapes the people who move through it.

We are drawn to natural materials, honest construction, and the kind of quiet elegance that reveals itself slowly. Our projects have been recognized by the European Prize for Architecture, the FAD Awards, and the Barcelona City Council.
